Exhausted, hungry, hurt. After being on the run for the last few days, [[Maelis Nighthollow|Maelis]] has found themselves in a dank, empty alley in a city they’ve never seen before. After hearing the stories about the surface and how ‘great’ it was, the last thing they needed is to be hunted after finally escaping that horrible place. This was supposed to be a fresh start, the beginning of a better life for them and for their [[Alye|Mother]], a way for them to finally be free of [[Filvrae Nighthollow|Him]] and all he’s done to them. They let out an exasperated sigh, before they collapse on the ground, and let out a quiet cry.

_I knew I couldn’t do it, why did I ever think I could escape, now I’m either gonna die or get captured by those stupid bounty hunters. I’m so sorry [[Alye|Mom]], it looks like I was never good enough to escape,_ _your death will all be for nothing_.

Just as they bring their head down to their hands, faint footsteps begin to approach them. Accepting their fate, they clean up their face, raise their head to face whoever is approaching and prepare themselves. To their surprise, the person who rounds the corner isn’t immediately hostile, but instead seems to be just as cautious at they are.
